What makes it Minimal?

The style reduces visual noise so the important information becomes easier to see.

FEEL

Calm · Focused · Precise

Minimalism works when reduction improves clarity. Empty space is not the goal by itself; every remaining element needs a reason to be there.

Empty ≠ Minimal

A sparse interface can still feel unfinished. Minimalism depends on strong decisions: hierarchy, rhythm, proportion, typography and intentional spacing.
